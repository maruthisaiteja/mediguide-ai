Pharmacokinetic Interaction Warnings

Simvastatin × Grapefruit Juice (CYP3A4 Inhibition)

Mechanism: Furanocoumarins in grapefruit irreversibly inhibit intestinal CYP3A4 enzymes.

Effect: Serum statin bioavailability surges by 200%–500%, causing rhabdomyolysis and acute kidney damage.

Clinical Action: AVOID grapefruit or pomelo completely while taking Simvastatin or Atorvastatin.

Warfarin × Alcohol (Hepatic Enzyme Competition)

Mechanism: Alcohol competes for CYP2C9 metabolic breakdown in liver hepatocytes.

Effect: Unpredictable spikes in INR ratio, leading to life-threatening internal gastrointestinal or cerebral hemorrhages.

Clinical Action: Strictly limit or avoid alcohol consumption while on Warfarin therapy.

Metformin × Alcohol (Lactic Acidosis)

Effect: Alcohol potentiates Metformin's effect on lactate metabolism, dramatically elevating fatal Lactic Acidosis risk.

Clinical Action: Avoid excessive alcohol consumption entirely.
